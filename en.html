What Is AllOne Net Firewall?

AllOne Net Firewall is an Android firewall app that lets you choose which apps are allowed to use the internet. Apps you tick are cut off, on both WiFi and mobile data.

Everything is set up on the device itself, with no root access and no external VPN server. Just pick your apps, then turn on the firewall.

Note: AllOne Net Firewall does not filter ads inside apps. It cuts all internet access for the apps you tick.

How Does It Work?

Local Android VPN

Android provides a VPN Service that apps can use to filter network traffic on the device. AllOne Net Firewall uses this capability to cut the connection of the apps you tick.

This is not a VPN service for changing your IP address or location. Traffic is not routed through any external VPN server run by the developer. Blocking happens on your device.

Good to Know

The key/VPN icon is normal

Android marks every VPN with a key icon in the status bar. This is a local VPN that runs the firewall on your device.

Other apps are not affected

Apps you haven't ticked are not affected, and their internet works as normal.

Only one VPN at a time

Android allows only one active VPN. If you turn on another VPN, this firewall stops and must be turned on again.

A “no connection” message is expected

A blocked app will show a no-connection message. That is exactly the point.

Confirmation for important apps

Before you block important phone apps (Google services, the Play Store, SystemUI, the phone app), the app asks for confirmation. Don't block important system apps unless you understand the consequences.

Auto start

The auto-start option in Settings turns the firewall back on after your phone restarts.

The tutorial can be reopened

Open More Features, then How to Use, or go through Settings, to see the 3-step tutorial and guide again.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AllOne Net Firewall?

AllOne Net Firewall is a no-root Android app that cuts internet access for the apps you choose, one app at a time, using Android's built-in local VPN Service.

No. The VPN Service is used only as a local firewall mechanism. It does not connect your device to an external VPN server and does not change your IP address or location.
